We’re building a new generation of intelligent insurance products across motor, medical, and life. Each product focuses on simplifying complex workflows, supporting informed decisions, and improving the experience for customers and insurance teams.

Motor insurance01
Planned product

BOIT Motor360

Move motor claims forward with less paperwork, coordinated reviews, and a clear case history for compliance checks.

Business benefits

  • Speed up claims from intake to resolution
  • Reduce manual document and image review
  • Flag potential fraud for earlier investigation
  • Give teams a clear view of every claim

Planned product

BOIT Medical360

Turn complex medical records into clear evidence so teams can review cases faster and apply policy rules consistently.

Business benefits

  • Reduce time spent reviewing medical records
  • Turn complex evidence into clear summaries
  • Support more consistent underwriting and claims reviews
  • Route exceptions to the right experts faster

  • Help teams respond to customer enquiries faster
  • Improve visibility across the policy lifecycle
